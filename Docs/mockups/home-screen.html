How it is applied

  • Coral means done or active. Completed checkbox, selected tab, the live trend line. Nothing else uses it.
  • One filled tile per row, never three. The two colour tiles sit against dark cards above and below so they stay loud.
  • Lavender marks a timer that is running — rules 4 and 7 for most of the day.
  • Text on any filled tile is #1A1712. White drops to roughly 1.5:1 on all three accents and is unreadable.
